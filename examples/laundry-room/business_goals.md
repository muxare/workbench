> **Invented example.** These goals, the cooperative and every person in it are made up for developing and demonstrating the workbench. Nothing here describes a real client.

The first two goals are the board's and came with the brief. The third is the residents': the residents' representative, Kim Example (invented), raised it at the 2026 spring meeting, and the board adopted it as a goal for the project on 2026-09-01 without discussing how it fits the other two.

It does not fit them easily. The board's way of reaching the first two goals is written into the brief: fault reports only through the new service, and, said in the kick-off but not written down, a booking ban for residents who leave three slots unused. The residents' representative opposes both. About a fifth of households are older residents without smartphones, who would lose the phone line, and a ban punishes people for a broken machine or a sick child as much as for forgetting. The `held_by` field says whose goal each one is, so a step can read from the front matter that the goals belong to two different stakeholders; how they conflict is still told only in this prose and in `stakeholder_context`. The field was added to the model on 2026-11-12, after the project, and filled in here from what this prose already said.
